# Product and Design Pack

Load for product flows, interaction behavior, visual hierarchy, prototypes, design implementation, or experience review.

## Defaults

- Ground decisions in the current source, running behavior, and rendered artifact; none substitutes for the other two.
- Use real or representative data and cover the states that shape the experience: loading, empty, populated, error, narrow viewport, and meaningful interaction edges as applicable.
- Prototype and browser-verify locally before changing an online surface when a preview-first sequence has been agreed.
- Preserve the approved shell, interaction rhythm, and selected visual direction. Do not replace the intended hierarchy with incidental polish or silently switch to an easier source variant.
- Compare against the human's stated intent and mental model, not merely against the implementation plan.

## Strong proof

Walk representative flows in the rendered runtime and capture the states where the product claim is visible. Visual quality requires visual evidence. A DOM measurement, green build, or code reading cannot by itself prove legibility, hierarchy, interaction feel, or mobile behavior.

When a visualization fails on a constrained surface, preserve the selected information direction and produce a concrete rendered alternative rather than declaring the concept complete from source.

## Completion sweep

Remove abandoned variants and temporary preview state unless comparison was part of the accepted outcome. Confirm the canonical artifact is obvious, responsive states remain usable, and no approved interaction mode was lost as a side effect.

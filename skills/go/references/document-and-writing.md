# Document and Writing Pack

Load for guides, specifications, reports, prose revisions, structured documents, and documentation changes.

## Defaults

- Recover the intended expression—what the human is trying to say—and the agreed edit scope before changing prose.
- Treat an approval to make one local change as exactly that. Preserve the document's accepted structure, voice, and neighboring content unless broader revision was explicitly included.
- Keep one source of truth. Link or consolidate repeated facts instead of creating parallel explanations that can drift.
- Write for the actual audience and task: enough context to understand without making the reader infer missing concepts, but no background warm-up or implementation diary.

## Strong proof

Read the changed section in context. When layout, hierarchy, export, links, tables, or visual grouping affect meaning, inspect the rendered or exported artifact; source text alone is insufficient. Verify that the intended expression survived the medium and that local changes did not alter unrelated claims.

## Completion sweep

Remove drafting residue, duplicated paragraphs, placeholder language, stale navigation, broken links, and claims no longer supported by the source of truth. Preserve unresolved content as an explicit decision or owned residual rather than smoothing it into false certainty.

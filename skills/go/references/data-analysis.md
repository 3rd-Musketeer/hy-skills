# Data Analysis Pack

Load for datasets, metrics, evaluations, analytical reports, experiments, or claims derived from data.

## Defaults

- State included data, exclusions, time range, population, provenance, and known gaps before drawing conclusions.
- Separate observation, interpretation, causal claim, and recommendation. Do not let confident prose erase uncertainty in the evidence.
- Check whether the conclusion outruns coverage. Missing context stays visible; it is not silently collapsed into a negative category or a universal claim.
- Preserve the evidence chain and reproducible query, transformation, or run artifact needed to audit material results.
- For personalized conclusions, explain why this evidence, why now, and why it relates to the subject; activity alone is not relevance.

## Strong proof

Re-run or independently inspect the query and validate totals, denominators, exclusions, representative rows, and boundary cases. Compare the written conclusion with the displayed distribution or underlying evidence, not only with an aggregate headline.

Report uncertainty and alternative explanations at the point where they constrain the decision. A reproducible calculation does not make an unsupported causal interpretation valid.

## Completion sweep

Remove stale exports and ambiguous intermediate datasets, or label and retain them when reproducibility requires it. Make the canonical input, output, parameters, and time cutoff readable from the artifact layout. Never expose protected raw content merely because aggregate audit access was authorized.

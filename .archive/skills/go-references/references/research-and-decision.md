# Research and Decision Pack

Load when the outcome is an evidence-backed recommendation, comparison, explanation, or decision rather than a changed system.

## Defaults

- Define the decision question, evaluation criteria, coverage boundary, and freshness requirement before searching.
- Prefer primary or authoritative sources. Re-query mutable facts instead of relying on old reports, screenshots, memory, or apparent consensus.
- Keep **fact**, **inference**, and **recommendation** visibly distinct. Say when a conclusion is an inference.
- Look for credible alternatives and counterevidence, especially evidence that could reverse the preferred option.
- Optimize for information gain: brief agreement or recap, then the judgment that changes what the reader should believe or do.

## Strong proof

Evidence should cover the material alternatives and support the exact decision criteria. Cite sources close to the claims they support. A recommendation needs a reason it wins under the stated constraints, not just a list of pros and cons.

Finish with the decision, remaining uncertainty, and a **revisit trigger**: the observable change that would make this conclusion worth reopening.

## Completion sweep

Remove duplicated notes and stale intermediate claims. Preserve only the evidence trail needed to reproduce the judgment. Do not create a durable research artifact unless the task needs one or the result must be cited again.
